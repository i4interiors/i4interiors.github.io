What we do

Services

Full-service design for homes and businesses — from first concept through to the final styled room.

Residential Design

Single rooms to whole-home renovations and new builds — space planning, finishes, millwork and lighting resolved as one cohesive scheme.

Commercial Design

Restaurants, retail, offices and hospitality. Interiors that hold up to daily use and still make your brand memorable.

Furniture Selection & Staging

Sourcing of furniture, rugs, lighting and art — then styled and placed on site, whether you are moving in or preparing to sell.

Design Consult

A focused session for clients who want direction, not a full project — layout, palette and a clear plan you can carry out yourself.
